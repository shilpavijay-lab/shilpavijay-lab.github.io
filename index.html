About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at Stanford's Center for Turbulence Research (CTR), where I work with Prof. Beverley McKeon on experimental investigations of turbulent heat transfer over rough surfaces and porous interfaces.

My research sits at the intersection of fluid mechanics, thermal engineering, and surface design. I build custom experimental systems — modular heated-wall test sections, cold-wire anemometry rigs, and IR thermography setups — to capture the physics that simulations miss.

Before Stanford, I earned my Ph.D. from USC (advisor: Prof. Mitul Luhar), where I demonstrated 200–400% enhancement in convective heat transfer through engineered porous surface architectures. I also hold a B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from College of Engineering Pune, India.

My work has been supported by the CTR Postdoctoral Fellowship, the USC Viterbi Graduate Fellowship, and the S.N. Bose Scholarship (top 55 students nationally in India, <5% acceptance rate).

Research & Projects

Thermal Footprints of Rough Walls

Investigating how turbulent boundary layers respond to step changes in surface roughness using infrared thermography and cold-wire anemometry. Mapping scalar internal boundary layer (IBL) growth and rough-to-smooth vs. smooth-to-rough transition asymmetries. Presented at APS DFD 2024 and THMT'25 Tokyo.

IR Thermography Cold-wire Anemometry Wind Tunnel Scalar IBL

Porous Surfaces for Passive Flow Control

Designed and fabricated engineered porous surface architectures achieving 200–400% enhancement in convective heat transfer. Used PIV, pressure sensing, and 500+ OpenFOAM simulations to map permeability-geometry relationships and optimize multiphysics performance via Bayesian optimization.

PIV OpenFOAM 3D Printing Bayesian Optimization

Design-by-Morphing Framework

Co-developed a multiphysics optimization framework that generates non-intuitive porous geometries from baseline topologies for thermofluid applications. Identified ~20% performance sensitivity to subtle topological features invisible to traditional parametric methods. Collaboration with Dr. Sangjoon Lee at CTR.

Topology Optimization CFD Porous Media
🛰️

SWAYAM Pico-Satellite (ISRO)

Designed passive magnetic attitude control components — magnetorquer rods and hysteresis dampers — for ISRO's SWAYAM student satellite using ANSYS Maxwell. Presented Critical Design Review at ISRO Satellite Center. Satellite successfully launched in 2016. Team recognized by the Prime Minister of India.
