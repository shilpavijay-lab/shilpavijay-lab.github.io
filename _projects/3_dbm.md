Built parameterized OpenFOAM + Bayesian optimization workflow in Python that automates simulation setup, execution, and post-processing across 500+ runs to explore high-dimensional geometry spaces.

Identified non-intuitive porous microstructure designs from 4 baseline topologies; mapped permeability--geometry response surfaces and quantified ~20% performance sensitivity to subtle topological features.

Collaboration with Dr. Sangjoon Lee at CTR.
