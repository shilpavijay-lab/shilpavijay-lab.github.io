Designed magnetorquer rods for the passive magnetic attitude control system of SWAYAM using ANSYS Maxwell. Presented Critical Design Review at ISRO Satellite Center, Bengaluru.

Satellite successfully launched by ISRO in 2016. Team felicitated by the Prime Minister of India.
