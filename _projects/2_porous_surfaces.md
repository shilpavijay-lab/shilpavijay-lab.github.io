Ph.D. thesis: leveraged turbulence at the interface of engineered porous surfaces for passive flow control, with applications to thermal transport and drag reduction.

Designed and built a custom partially-porous channel flow facility with thermal management, pressure taps, and PIV optical access; demonstrated 200--400% enhancement in convective heat transfer across 15+ porous substrate configurations.

Fabricated anisotropic porous lattice substrates via SLA 3D printing; first-of-its-kind experimental validation of resolvent-based porous surface designs.
